# QIF Import Feature - Quick Summary

## ✅ **FEATURE COMPLETE!**

Your Personal Finance Tracker now supports importing QIF files!

---

## 🎯 **What You Can Do**

Import transactions from:
- ✅ **Bank exports** (checking, savings accounts)
- ✅ **Credit card statements**
- ✅ **Quicken** software
- ✅ **Microsoft Money**
- ✅ **Any QIF-compatible software**

---

## 🚀 **How to Use (3 Easy Steps)**

### **Step 1: Get Your QIF File**
- Export from your bank or financial software
- Look for "Export to Quicken" or "QIF format"

### **Step 2: Import**
1. Go to **Settings** page
2. Scroll to **"Import QIF File"** section
3. Select the **account** to import into
4. Choose your **QIF file**
5. Click **"Parse"** to preview

### **Step 3: Confirm**
- Review the transaction preview
- Click **"Import X Transactions"**
- Done! ✅

---

## 📊 **What Gets Imported**

From each transaction:
- ✅ Date
- ✅ Amount (income/expense)
- ✅ Payee
- ✅ Description/Memo
- ✅ Category (auto-mapped)
- ✅ Check number (if present)

## 🎨 **Features**

- ✅ **Preview before import** - See transactions first

- ✅ **Category mapping** - Auto-maps to your categories

## ⚠️ **Important Notes**

1. **No duplicate checking** - Don't import the same file twice
2. **Select correct account** - All transactions go to one account
3. **Review after import** - Check transactions are correct
4. **Categories auto-mapped** - May need manual adjustment
