# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - Finance Tracker

Get started with Finance Tracker in just a few minutes!

## Option 1: Try with Sample Data (Recommended for First-Time Users)

1. **Open the Application**
   - The app will show a welcome screen if you're a first-time user

2. **Load Sample Data**
   - Click the "Load Sample Data" button on the welcome screen
   - This will populate the app with realistic example data

3. **Explore the Features**
   - Check the Dashboard to see your financial overview
   - Browse Accounts to see different account types
   - View Transactions to see sample income and expenses
   - Check Budgets to see how budget tracking works
   - Look at Recurring transactions for automated entries
   - Generate Reports to see analytics

4. **Start Fresh When Ready**
   - Go to Settings
   - Click "Clear All Data"
   - Begin adding your own accounts and transactions

## Option 2: Start Fresh

### Step 1: Add Your First Account

1. Click on **"Accounts"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Add Account"** button
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Name**: e.g., "Main Checking"
   - **Type**: Select account type (Checking, Savings, etc.)
   - **Balance**: Enter current balance
   - **Currency**: Select your currency (default: USD)
   - **Color**: Pick a color for easy identification
4. Click **"Add Account"**

### Step 2: Add More Accounts

Repeat Step 1 for all your accounts:
- Checking accounts
- Savings accounts
- Credit cards (balance will show as negative)
- Investment accounts
- Cash

### Step 3: Record Your First Transaction

1. Click on **"Transactions"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Add Transaction"** button
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Type**: Income, Expense, or Transfer
   - **Account**: Select the account
   - **Amount**: Enter the amount
   - **Category**: Choose appropriate category
   - **Description**: Add details (optional)
   - **Date**: Select transaction date
4. Click **"Add Transaction"**

### Step 4: Set Up Budgets

1. Click on **"Budgets"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Add Budget"** button
3. Configure your budget:
   - **Name**: e.g., "Monthly Groceries"
   - **Category**: Select expense category
   - **Amount**: Set budget limit
   - **Period**: Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly
   - **Alert Threshold**: Set warning percentage (e.g., 80%)
4. Click **"Add Budget"**

### Step 5: Add Recurring Transactions

1. Click on **"Recurring"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Add Recurring"** button
3. Set up the recurring transaction:
   - **Type**: Income, Expense, or Transfer
   - **Amount**: Enter amount
   - **Category**: Choose category
   - **Description**: Add details
   - **Frequency**: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.
   - **Start Date**: When it begins
   - **End Date**: When it ends (optional)
4. Click **"Add Recurring Transaction"**

### Step 6: View Your Dashboard

1. Click on **"Dashboard"** in the sidebar
2. See your financial overview:
   - Total balance across all accounts
   - Monthly income and expenses
   - Recent transactions
   - Account balances

### Step 7: Generate Reports

1. Click on **"Reports"** in the sidebar
2. Choose report type:
   - **Monthly Summary**: View specific month
   - **Date Range**: Custom date range analysis
   - **Cash Flow**: Account-specific cash flow
3. Analyze your financial data

## 🔄 Data Management

### Export Your Data:
1. Go to **Settings**
2. Click **"Export to JSON"**
3. Save the file to a safe location
4. Do this monthly for backups

### Import Data:
1. Go to **Settings**
2. Click **"Import from JSON"**
3. Select your backup file
4. Confirm the import

### Clear Data:
1. Go to **Settings**
2. Click **"Clear All Data"**
3. Confirm (this cannot be undone!)
4. Start fresh

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Data Storage:
- All data is stored locally in your browser
- Clearing browser data will delete your financial records
- Export regularly to keep backups
- Data doesn't sync across browsers/devices

### Privacy:
- Your data never leaves your device
- No account or login required
- Completely private and secure

### Browser Compatibility:
- Works best on modern browsers
- Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge
- Keep your browser updated

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Data Not Saving:
- Check if browser allows LocalStorage
- Don't use private/incognito mode
- Check browser storage settings

### Balance Incorrect:
- Review all transactions
- Check for duplicate entries
- Verify transfer transactions

### Budget Not Updating:
- Ensure transactions are categorized correctly
- Check budget period (monthly/quarterly/yearly)
- Verify transaction dates

### Can't See Data:
- Check if you're in the right browser
- Verify LocalStorage is enabled
- Try refreshing the page
