## How It Works Now

### Selecting Accounts
1. **Default:** All accounts selected (all buttons gray)
2. **Click "Main Checking":** Selects ONLY Main Checking (button turns blue)
3. **Threshold lines appear** for Main Checking
4. **Click "Main Checking" again:** Deselects it (back to All)
5. **Click another account:** Switches to that account

### Threshold Lines
- **Only show when ONE account is selected**
- **Red dashed line** = Low balance warning
- **Yellow dashed line** = High balance warning
- **Labels show exact amounts**
- **Legend explains the lines**

### Date Display
- **Correct month and year**
- **Consistent format** across all periods
- **Shows current month dates** when applicable
- **Clear timeline** from Now to end of forecast
